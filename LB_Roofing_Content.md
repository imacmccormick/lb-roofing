## 3. Services (Residential & Commercial)

### Roofing Systems
* **Asphalt Shingles:** Affordable, versatile, and dependable protection in many styles.
* **EPDM Rubber:** Durable, weather-resistant membrane for flat roofs.
* **TPO:** Energy-efficient white membrane that reflects heat and reduces cooling costs.
* **Metal Roofing:** Long-lasting and highly durable for harsh weather conditions.
* **Silicone Roof Coating:** Liquid-applied seal to stop leaks and extend roof life without full replacement.

### Exterior Solutions
* **Seamless Gutters:** Custom-fit to reduce leaks and protect against water damage.
* **Siding (All Types):** Enhance curb appeal and protection with quality vinyl or designer siding.
* **Windows (Vinyl & Designer):** Improve energy efficiency and insulation with modern window styles.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does a roof last?**
A: Most roofs last around 25–30 years. Factors like weather, maintenance, and seasonal changes can impact this lifespan.

**Q: Do you help with insurance claims?**
A: Yes! We handle the process from beginning to end—from the initial inspection to meeting with adjusters and documenting damage.

**Q: How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?**
A: Look for leaks, granule loss, cracked shingles, or lifting. The best way to know is a professional inspection—call us for a free check-up.
